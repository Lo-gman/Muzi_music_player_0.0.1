Press play on what you already love.
Muzi Music turns the songs on your phone into a polished listening experience, with a minimal design that keeps your music front and centre. Open it, and your library is already there.

✦ Effortless from the first tap

Muzi finds your music the moment you open it. Call recordings, voice notes, ringtones and alarms are skipped, so only real music makes the cut.

✦ A minimal player worth looking at

Big, bold cover art, a smooth seek bar, shuffle, repeat and favorites, all on a clean, uncluttered screen. Swipe between Now Playing, Library and Settings with fluid, graceful transitions.

✦ Always within reach
Keep listening with the screen off.
Control your music from the notification, lock screen or headphone buttons.
Pin a minimal dark or light home-screen widget and skip tracks without opening the app.
Calls pause your music, and it resumes when they end.
Unplug your headphones and the music pauses.
Muzi remembers exactly where you stopped.

✦ Your library, your way

Search instantly, sort by name, recent or artist, or show only your favorites. Hold a song’s name to share or delete it. Hold its cover to rename the song, change the artist or give it new artwork.

✦ Sound that’s yours

A 5-band equalizer with Flat, Bass, Vocal, Treble and Rock presets, so every track sounds the way you like.

✦ Dressed for your mood

Four themes: Dark, Light, Cyan and Purple.

✦ Respectful by design

Everything stays on your device. Muzi has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Deleting a song or editing its details only changes Muzi’s library, and your original files are never touched.

Muzi Music. Minimal. Stylish. Yours.
Works on Android 5.0 and newer.
